# Privacy Policy for VenueVerse

Effective Date: 5 August 2026

VenueVerse is a college venue booking application developed for Sri Ramakrishna Engineering College. This Privacy Policy explains how the app collects, uses, stores, and protects user information.

## Information We Collect

VenueVerse may collect the following information:

- Name
- Email address
- Department
- User role
- Venue booking details
- Event title and event type
- Faculty coordinator details
- Booking approval, rejection, cancellation, and revocation records
- Receipt and QR verification details
- Firebase Cloud Messaging device token for push notifications
- Hall images uploaded by authorised admins

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Allow staff and authorised users to sign in
- Manage venue booking requests
- Allow department admins to approve, reject, or revoke bookings
- Generate booking receipts
- Verify receipt QR codes
- Send booking-related notifications
- Manage venues, users, departments, and app roles
- Improve app reliability and security

## Google Sign-In

VenueVerse uses Google Sign-In for authentication. Only authorised institutional staff accounts may access the app. Student and non-institutional accounts may be restricted.

## Push Notifications

VenueVerse uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to send notifications related to:

- Booking requests
- Booking approval or rejection
- Booking revocation
- Receipt generation
- Receipt email status
- Important venue or account updates

## Camera Access

VenueVerse may request camera access for QR code scanning and venue image capture. Camera access is used only for these app features.

## Data Storage

VenueVerse uses Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend functions. Booking receipts, venue information, user profiles, and notification records may be stored securely in Supabase.

## Data Sharing

VenueVerse does not sell user data.

Data may be processed through the following services used by the app:

- Supabase
- Firebase Cloud Messaging
- Google Sign-In
- Gmail SMTP or email service for receipt delivery

These services are used only to provide app functionality.

## Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information through authentication, role-based access control, database policies, and secure backend functions.

## Data Retention

Booking records, receipts, and related logs may be retained for administrative, audit, and verification purposes. Some temporary receipt PDF files may be cleaned up periodically.

## Account and Data Deletion

Users may request account or data deletion by contacting the app administrator.

## Children’s Privacy

VenueVerse is intended for authorised college users and staff. It is not intended for children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page.
