Privacy Policy for VenueVerse

Effective date: 6 August 2026

VenueVerse is a college venue booking and schedule management application designed for authorised users of Sri Ramakrishna Engineering College.

Information We Collect

  • Name and institutional email address
  • Department and assigned app role
  • Venue booking and event information
  • Booking approval, rejection, cancellation and revocation records
  • Receipt and QR verification information
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging device tokens
  • Venue images uploaded by authorised administrators

How We Use Information

Information is used to authenticate users, manage venue booking requests, process approvals, generate receipts, verify QR codes, deliver notifications and maintain application security.

Google Sign-In

VenueVerse may use Google Sign-In for authentication. Access is restricted to authorised institutional accounts and approved staff.

Push Notifications

Firebase Cloud Messaging is used to send booking request, approval, rejection, revocation, receipt and other operational notifications.

Camera Access

Camera access may be used for scanning receipt QR codes and capturing venue images. VenueVerse does not use the camera for background monitoring.

Third-Party Services

VenueVerse uses the following services:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage and backend functions
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications
  • Google Sign-In for supported authentication
  • Email services for delivering booking receipt copies

Data Sharing

VenueVerse does not sell user information. Information is shared only with service providers required to operate the application.

Data Retention

Booking, receipt and audit records may be retained for institutional administration, verification and security purposes. Temporary receipt files may be deleted periodically.

Account and Data Deletion

Users may request account or personal-data deletion by contacting: venueverse.srec@gmail.com

Children's Privacy

VenueVerse is intended for authorised adult institutional users and is not directed toward children.
